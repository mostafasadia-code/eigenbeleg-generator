What is a Substitute Receipt?

A self-issued document that proves a business expense when the original receipt is missing. It is a last resort, not a replacement for a proper invoice.

  • 1Ask for a duplicate first. Most merchants, hotels and taxi apps can resend a receipt.
  • 2No VAT deduction. Parloa can’t reclaim input VAT on a substitute receipt, so the company bears the full gross amount.
  • 3Be precise and truthful. Date, payee, amount and purpose must match the transaction exactly.
  • 4Add evidence if you can, e.g. a bank or card statement, a booking confirmation or an email.
